    Keyboard shortcuts

    Move

    → ↓
    Next card. After the last card of a substep, goes to the first card of the next one. After the last card overall, opens the review list.
    ← ↑
    Previous card. From the first card of a substep, goes to the last card of the one before. From the review list, returns to the last card.
    Arrow buttons
    Same as → and ← (the sidebar dots show where you are in a substep).
    1–9
    Jump to the first substep of step 1 to 9.
    p
    Replay the diagram animation.

    Marks and notes

    s
    Mark the current check or question Safe. Press again to reopen it.
    r
    Mark it Review, so it goes on the review list. Press again to reopen it.
    n
    Open or close the note box on this card.
    g
    On the review list: publish the pending review (asks first; one PENDING review per PR).
    Esc
    Close the note box, the substep picker, or this list.

    Other

    t
    Cycle the theme: System, Light, Dark.
    ?
    Show or hide this list.

    Shortcuts are off while you type in a note.
